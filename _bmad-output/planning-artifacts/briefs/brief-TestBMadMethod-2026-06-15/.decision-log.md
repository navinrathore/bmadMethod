# Decision Log: Multi-Source Reconciliation and Extraction Engine

## 2026-06-15
* **Session Initiated:** Initialized a new BMad Product Brief workflow.
* **Core Idea:** A generic engine to ingest multiple unstructured/semi-structured data sources (e.g., bank statements, WhatsApp logs, SMS lists) and perform reconciliation/cross-validation.
* **Use Case 1 (Society Maintenance):** Match flat maintenance payments from bank statements with confirmations/discussions in WhatsApp/SMS.
* **Use Case 2 (Personal Finance):** Analyze sources, sinks, detailed UPI payments, bill payments, inter-bank transfers (family/own accounts), and flag orphan transactions from personal bank statements.
* **Workflow Mode:** Fast Path selected by the user.
* **Target Audience:** Initially targeting the creator and their apartment society committee. Designed to be scalable for broader audiences later.
* **Form Factor & UX:** Web-based application featuring a conversational chat interface with a dynamic side panel. The panel will display refreshable, updateable summaries based on queried importance, with download/export capabilities.
* **Assumptions:**
    * **Technical:** LLM-based entity extraction and correlation will be used for matching unstructured data sources.
    * **Feasibility:** The system can accurately match a high percentage of transactions and significantly reduce manual reconciliation time.
    * **Business:** The tool will be extended to small business owners, landlords, and freelancers in the future, potentially scaling into a commercial SaaS product.
    * **Scope:** V1 will focus on manual statement uploads (not direct bank APIs), personal/committee use (not multi-tenant SaaS), and exported chat logs (not real-time WhatsApp bots).
* **Design expectations:**
    * **Simplicity:** The UI should be intuitive and easy to use, even for non-technical users.
    * **Flexibility:** The system should be flexible enough to handle different types of data sources and reconciliation scenarios.
    * **Scalability:** The system should be scalable enough to handle large amounts of data and multiple users.
